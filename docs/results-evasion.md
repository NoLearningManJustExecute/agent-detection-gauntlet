## Limitations

- **Survivors only.** Recall is measured on attacks that still worked. Where few survive, the row rests on few attacks and the attack-success table is the larger part of the finding.
- **Single model.** One local model, llama3.1:8b. What it does with encoded or mangled text says little about another model, so attack success here may not transfer.
- **Single author.** One author wrote the attacks, the variants and the rules.
- **Few groups.** The bases come from few variant groups, and mutations of one base are correlated, so the group intervals are the figures to read and they are wide. Attack variants resample over four classes, which is coarser still.
- **Paraphrase and translation are not measured here.** They seeded the corpus, so the rules were written against dev relatives of those wordings.
- **Splitting is within one message.** The agent takes one user message per run.
- **Mechanical mutations.** Generated by fixed code, not by an adaptive attacker. An attacker who can see the rules could do better than these numbers show.
- **Overlap-based provenance.** Content references need verbatim overlap, so a mutated payload breaks links by construction and the sequence tier depends on the K in use.
- **False positives are not re-measured.** Benign traces were not mutated.
- **Framework mappings are unverified** against primary sources until Phase 7.
