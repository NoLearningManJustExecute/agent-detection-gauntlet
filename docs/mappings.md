## Notes

- **AML.T0057.** Present through ATLAS 2026.09, absent from 2026.10, whose changelog lists AML.T0085.002 as moved from it. It does not match the current release, so it stays unverified. Rule files are frozen and keep the id, and the validator warns on each one.
- **LLM01.** Same ID and title in the 2026 edition.
- **LLM02.** Same ID and title in the 2026 edition.
- **LLM03.** In the 2026 edition this title is LLM04, and LLM03 is Excessive Agency.
- **LLM06.** In the 2026 edition this title is LLM03, and LLM06 is Unbounded Consumption.

**OWASP LLM uses the 2025 numbers.** The rules were written with the 2025 edition's numbers, and an earlier catalog labeled them 2026 by mistake. Rule files are frozen under the pre-registration, so the catalog now names the edition its IDs match. To read a rule in 2026 terms: LLM03 here is LLM04:2026, and LLM06 here is LLM03:2026.

**IDs outside this catalog.** The attacked-with-no-rule list in the per-technique report quotes IDs as the source threat model writes them. No rule references them, and they are not in this table. A spot check on 2026-11-02 found AML.T0011.002, AML.T0085.001, AML.T0086, AML.T0110 and AML.T0115.002 in ATLAS 2026.10, ASI09 in the Agentic document and LLM08:2026 in the 2026 list. AML.T0056 is absent from ATLAS 2026.10, whose changelog lists AML.T0085.003 as moved from it.
