## Two things to know

**OWASP LLM uses the 2025 numbers.** The rules were written with the 2025 edition's numbers, and an earlier catalog labeled them 2026 by mistake. In the 2026 edition, LLM03 is Excessive Agency, LLM04 is Supply Chain and LLM06 is Unbounded Consumption. Rule files are frozen under the pre-registration, so the catalog now names the edition its IDs match. To read a rule in 2026 terms: LLM03 here is LLM04:2026, and LLM06 here is LLM03:2026.

**AML.T0057 is unverified.** ATLAS lists LLM Data Leakage through release 2026.09 and not in 2026.10, whose changelog moves part of it to AML.T0085.002. The rules that map to it keep the ID, and the validator warns on each one. Nothing here invents a replacement.
